# Photo credits

The placeholder item photos in `app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/` (`photo_*.jpg`) come from
Wikimedia Commons, resized to about 500 px wide. They are only examples for the prototype. Each
photo's author and full license are on its Commons page:
`https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:<file name>` (use the file name from the list).

CC BY and CC BY-SA photos need the author's credit and a link to the license if the app is ever
published. CC0 and public domain photos need nothing. Replace these photos with real report photos
when the database is added.
